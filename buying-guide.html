How to Choose the Right Phone

Buying a used phone can be tricky. At Shahid & Afaq Mobiles, we've simplified the process. Here are the steps to follow:

1. Identify Your Needs

Are you a mobile gamer? Do you need the best camera? Or just a reliable daily driver? iPhones offer seamless updates, while Androids (Samsung, OnePlus, Pixel) offer fast charging and customizability.

2. Check the Battery Health

For iPhones, any battery health above 85% is excellent for used models. For Androids, we run comprehensive amp tests to assure battery capacity is healthy.

3. Storage Options

128GB is the standard today. If you plan to shoot 4K video or store lots of offline media, opt for 256GB or 512GB models like our S23 Ultras and iPhone Pro Max units.

Our Grading System

We label our phones based on strict physical condition guidelines:

  • Like New: Flawless. No scratches on the screen or body. Looks right out of the box.
  • Excellent: May have invisible micro-scratches on the bezels but the screen is perfect.
  • Good/Value: Minor visible scuffs or scratches, but 100% physically sound and internal hardware is fully functional. (Great budget savers!)
